PowerPoint Live Results
Show live poll results inside your presentation deck
What this does
The PollSlide PowerPoint Add-in embeds a live results pane directly inside PowerPoint. As audience members submit answers on their phones, the chart updates in real time — right on your slide, no switching windows.
1
Install the Add-in
In PowerPoint, go to Insert → Get Add-ins and search for PollSlide. Click Add. It will appear in your Home ribbon.
💡 Sideload for testing: In PowerPoint → Insert → Add-ins → Upload My Add-in → select powerpoint-manifest.xml from your PollSlide download.
2
Launch a Question
In PollSlide Presenter (this page), click a question in the sidebar to go live. The question becomes active and its Session Code is shown below.
No question live yet — launch one first.
3
Connect in PowerPoint
Open the PollSlide panel inside PowerPoint. Paste the Session Code (or the full results URL) into the add-in. The live chart will appear immediately and update as responses come in.
4
Present — Results Update Live
Run your slideshow normally. The PollSlide panel shows the results chart in real time. Click Reveal here in Presenter to highlight correct answers — the add-in updates instantly.
Alternative: Embed in Browser / OBS / Companion
You can also embed the live results view in any browser window, OBS Studio overlay, or the PollSlide Keynote companion app using this URL format:
https://app.pollslide.com/answer?session=YOUR_CODE
↑ Click to copy · Replace YOUR_CODE with your session code
