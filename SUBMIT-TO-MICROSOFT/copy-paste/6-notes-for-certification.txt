PollSlide LIVE is a PowerPoint CONTENT add-in: it is inserted onto a slide and shows live audience results there, including during Slide Show.

GETTING AN ACCOUNT
This is NOT an enterprise-only add-in. Any individual can create a free account at app.pollslide.com/presenter in about a minute — no enterprise purchase, licence key or administrator provisioning, and no credit card for the free plan. Optional paid plans raise limits. The add-in says this on its first screen. Support: help@pollslide.com · https://pollslide.com/powerpoint-live

FIRST RUN
In editing view the object explains what it does and offers sign-in. During Slide Show it shows results only, with no sign-in prompt: results are readable without an account, and a login box on a slide in front of an audience would not be usable.

ACCOUNT LINKS — SIGN IN, SIGN UP, SIGN OUT (policy 1100.5.7.1)
- Sign in: the first screen in editing view.
- Sign up: "Create a free account" link on that same screen. It opens the PollSlide sign-up form (app.pollslide.com/presenter?signup=1) in the browser.
- Sign out: once signed in, "Signed in as <email> · Sign out" is shown under the presentation list and the question list, and a "Sign out" button is on the toolbar of a slide that already shows a question (editing view). Signing out returns to the sign-in screen.
Slide Show shows results only, with no account controls (see FIRST RUN).

HOW TO TEST (about 2 minutes) — PowerPoint on the web is quickest
1. Insert > Add-ins > My Add-ins > PollSlide LIVE. It is inserted ONTO the slide as an object (not a task pane).
2. Click the object and sign in with the test account above.
3. Choose "Reviewer Demo", then any multiple-choice question.
4. To answer as the audience: open https://app.pollslide.com/presenter in another browser tab (same test account), open "Reviewer Demo", and open the question's live view — it shows the QR code and the answer link. Open that link (or scan the QR with a phone) and submit an answer.
5. The count and bars on the slide update within a second or two.
6. Start the Slide Show — the object keeps showing and updating the results.
7. Leave the Slide Show. Click "Sign out" on the object's toolbar — you are back on the sign-in screen.
