How it works

1. Build your polls

Create questions at app.pollslide.com and launch a live session.

2. Insert a QR code

Select a question from the Polls tab → click Insert QR to slide. The QR is placed on your current slide.

3. Auto-detect (recommended)

As you navigate slides, PollSlide scans for QR codes automatically. The live results panel opens the moment a poll slide is detected — no manual step needed.

4. Reveal answers

The correct answer stays hidden until the countdown fires (or you click Reveal). Works in Presenter View too — the task pane is always visible on your laptop.
