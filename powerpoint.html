How to use
1. Build your polls
Create questions at app.pollslide.com
2. Insert a QR code
Select a question in the Polls tab, then click "Insert QR into slide". PowerPoint will add the QR to your current slide.
3. Present & watch results
During your presentation, click "Live" tab to see responses in real time. Your audience scans the QR with their phone.
4. Reveal the answer
The correct answer is hidden until the countdown fires (or you click Reveal now). Then confetti! 🎉
