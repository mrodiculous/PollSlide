## Long description (~150 words)
> PollSlide is live audience engagement that works over the slides you already have — no rebuilding your deck in another tool. Paste a QR code onto any slide in Keynote, PowerPoint, Google Slides or a PDF; your audience scans it and answers on their own phones, with no app to install and no account to create.
>
> One plan covers the whole suite: live polls and word clouds, graded quizzes with game modes (speed rounds, streaks, survival, wager rounds and team play), async surveys, audience Q&A with upvoting, flashcards with spaced repetition, and LoopSlide for trivia on venue TVs. Polly, the built-in AI, drafts questions from a topic or your own material.
>
> The free Mac companion floats live results over any presentation and follows your slides automatically. Teachers get class rosters, verified students, retakes and a gradebook export for Google Classroom and Canvas. Free to start; paid plans from $12/month.

## Comparison phrasing (for "alternatives" sites like AlternativeTo and SaaSHub)
Say: "an alternative to Mentimeter, Slido, Poll Everywhere, Kahoot, AhaSlides and Wooclap that works over
your existing slides." **Don't** claim to be "better" or "the best". Each site lets users vote; the
comparison pages on pollslide.com (vs-*) carry the honest detail.
